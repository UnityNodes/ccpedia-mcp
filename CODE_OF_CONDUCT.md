# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be direct about the work, not about the person. Disagree with a claim or
a design decision as hard as you want; do not make it personal.

## Expected

- Report problems precisely: what you asked, what tool ran, what came
  back, what you expected instead. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- Assume good faith on a first pass. Most wrong answers here are a
  missing or stale source, not carelessness.
- Stay on topic: this repository covers the hosted MCP server, its
  tools, and the data it serves.

## Not acceptable

- Personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory language or imagery,
  in issues, discussions, or pull requests.
- Publishing someone else's private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.
- Sexualized language or imagery in any project space.

## Scope

This applies to every space this repository controls: issues,
discussions, pull requests, and any other channel where you are
representing this project.

## Enforcement

Report a violation to security@unitynodes.com or through the
[contact form](https://ccpedia.xyz/contact). Reports are handled
privately. A maintainer may remove content, close threads, or ban a
participant, temporarily or permanently, depending on severity.
